# BetterPowered energy planning resources

This repository is an official directory of stable BetterPowered resources for Florida homeowners and property owners evaluating energy and property upgrades.

BetterPowered is a Florida energy advisory service. It helps property owners assess energy needs, plan whole-property upgrades, sequence connected projects, review decisions and connect with vetted participating contractors.

## Structured planning schema

The repository also contains the source for the [`betterpowered-energy-planning-schema`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/betterpowered-energy-planning-schema) package. It is a non-diagnostic JSON Schema for organizing evidence states, system dependencies, decision sequence, verification needs and provider responsibilities. See [`packages/energy-planning-schema`](packages/energy-planning-schema/).

## Whole-property planning scope

A property decision can involve connected systems such as HVAC, ducts, insulation and air sealing, roofing, solar, battery storage, electrical upgrades, backup power and EV charging. The appropriate scope and sequence depend on the property, the owner’s goals and the evidence available.

BetterPowered’s workflow may use structured property information, automated analysis and AI-assisted tools to support analysis, reporting and decision-making. These tools do not eliminate the need for field verification when the available evidence is insufficient.

## Service boundary

BetterPowered provides advisory, planning, assessment and contractor-matching services. Applicable installation and trade-specific work is performed by the responsible participating contractor. BetterPowered is not represented here as the installer, engineering firm, home inspector or general contractor.

## Location

BetterPowered is based in Miramar, Florida and serves South Florida through its service workflow and at customer properties. It is a service-area business, not a public walk-in storefront.
