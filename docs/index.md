# BetterPowered energy planning resources

BetterPowered is a Florida energy advisory service helping homeowners and property owners assess energy needs, plan whole-property upgrades, sequence connected projects, review decisions and connect with vetted participating contractors.

This documentation is an official, maintained directory for BetterPowered's planning methodology and stable service resources. It supports the primary website; it does not replace a property-specific scope, field verification, contractor proposal or licensed diagnosis.

## Operating scope

BetterPowered provides energy advisory, whole-property planning, defined-scope assessments, analysis, upgrade sequencing, incentive research, decision support and contractor matching. Participating contractors remain responsible for applicable installation, trade diagnosis, licences, permits, warranties and project contracts.

BetterPowered is based in Miramar, Florida and serves customers through its service workflow and at their properties. It is a service-area business, not a public walk-in storefront.

## Technology layer

BetterPowered may use structured property information, automated analysis, artificial intelligence, machine-learning and vision-assisted workflows to support analysis, reporting and decision-making. These tools support the advisory workflow; they do not create autonomous engineering conclusions or eliminate field verification when evidence is insufficient.
