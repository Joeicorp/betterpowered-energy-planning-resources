# Evidence and provider boundaries

A useful report states both what it supports and what it cannot yet establish.

## Evidence states

- **Observed or documented:** supported by accessible conditions, owner records or named measurements.
- **Reported:** supplied by the property owner or another party but not independently verified in the current scope.
- **Inferred:** a planning hypothesis that should be tested before commitment.
- **Not assessed:** inaccessible, outside the written scope or reserved for a qualified specialist.
